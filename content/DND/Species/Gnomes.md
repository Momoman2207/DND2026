Working closely alongside and under the guise of [[Dwarves]], Gnomes are silly little guys that love nothing more than to tinker and build. For this reason they are often seen working in mines repairing equipment or in the halls of [[The Engineering Guild]]'s facilities. Working so closely with [[Dwarves]] means that they have all but forgotten Gnomish and instead can be found speaking dwarvish.
