Two main species of elves exist on [[Adrios]]: High Elves and Dark Elves. These species of Elves very rarely interact with each other, and don't know other species exist.

**High Elves**
High Elves are commonly seen in the higher echelon of the cities of [[Humans]], and are sparsely seen elsewhere. They are very rare, with only about 1,000 still existing, and are on course to die out in the next few generations. High Elves value the finer things in life and would rather die that be separated from their parties and wines.

**Dark Elves**
The Dark Elves are very rarely seen, often keeping to themselves deep within the [[Whitefall Mountains]]. When they are seen, they are often alone and exploring around, usually finding themselves in adventuring parties. Many don't speak common and if they do they speak a very broken variation.
