Orcs are a simple people with love for two things: a good scrap and a good meal. Anything they can do to get closer to those two things they will do. Orcs are hostile towards all other citizens of [[Adrios]], even themselves, so if a good scrap is nowhere in sight, they will make one, even among themselves if necessary. This has shunned them out of the societies of other races. The Orcs have taken this offensive, and have as such decided to attack these civilized peoples before anything else.

As a result of Orc raids on convoys across [[The Great Plains]], the residents have overwhelmingly adopted airships as the main form of transportation across the land as to not get raided.
