The Banking Union processes all valuable materials mined in [[Oberon]], storing them or turning them into currency which is then circulated across [[Adrios]]. The Banking Union holds massive influence through it's role in storing and distributing valuables, making it the bank of choice for not just [[Dwarves]], but elves and humans as well. The Banking Union is also responsible for taxes within [[Oberon]], and manages the bank account for the city and all of it's public expenditures, which are decided on by the Council as a whole. At the head of the Banking Union is the Vault Keeper, Duff Bright. He manages a large portion of the wealth on [[Adrios]].
