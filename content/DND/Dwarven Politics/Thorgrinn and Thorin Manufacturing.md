T&T Manufacturing is the dominant manufacturing entity within [[Oberon]] and most of [[Adrios]]. T&T makes almost anything conceivable, from raw Iron or Steel to Rails and trains to mechanical mining suits. They are such a powerhouse by modern day largely because of sheer scale. District 3 spans 5 large peaks within the [[Whitefall Mountains]]. These peaks are known as the Khelda mountains, located roughly central within the mountain range at large.

T&T are responsible for all travel between the districts, whether from district to district, district to mine site, or vice versa. This centralization of the rail network makes it quite fast, being able to cross the entire mountain range in 10 hours. Quite the impressive feat. T&T are also in charge of exports and imports because of this logistical prowess.

Thorgrinn and Thorin are hard at work at [[The Council]], and don't have much time for personal oversight of the factory floor, so have created a vast network of managers of the factory floor, known as overseers.
