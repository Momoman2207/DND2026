Occupying District 2, The engineering Guild is responsible for the innovation of [[Oberon]]. Little is known of them, as they keep very much to themselves, but they are often on the leading edge of technology. Because of the Engineering guild, the [[Dwarves]] had their industrial revolution 60 years prior to humans and elves.

Within the Engineering guild there are a select secret few who dabble in the taboo. These Tech Priests are Engineers who spend their time researching the arcane.

The Archmechanist Belisarius Cawl is a half mechanical dwarf with a gigantic red lens left eye walking on a four legged contraption. He was appointed Archmechanist 13 years prior to the events of the campaign for his innovations on the steam engine.
