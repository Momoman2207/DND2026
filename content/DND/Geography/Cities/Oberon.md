Oberon is the largest "City" in the world, spanning 650 miles across from east to the west coast of the southern coast of [[Adrios]]. It comprises 14 million [[Dwarves]] and 3 million [[Gnomes]], making it the largest in both numbers and size. Oberon is not a normal city, but instead a collection of 273 individual cities, called districts, dotted around the [[Whitefall Mountains]].

Oberon is also the greatest producer of materials in weight and quality on [[Adrios]], producing Gold, Silver, Coal, Bauxite, gemstones, and many other rare metals and gemstones.

**Districts**
Oberon is split into main districts, each being under a different mountain within the range, with. Each district has a main role within the larger city, and they are separated by great stone gates that act as an entrance from the large rail network that interweaves the [[Whitefall Mountains]]

District 1:
District 1 is a place of incomprehensible opulence and grandeur, populated by [[The Enclave]], [[The Ministry of Enlightenment]], [[The Banking Union]], and the members of [[The Council]]. District 1 resides under Mt. Valtan, the crown jewel of the mountain range.

District 2:
District 2 is the district of [[The Engineering Guild]]. It is characterized by forges and workshops connected by a complex small scale rail network. little is known about it's inhabitants.

District 3: 
Ruled by [[Thorgrinn and Thorin Manufacturing]], District 3 is the largest district in area, and makes everything that the [[dwarves]] use. Forges smelt metric tons of Iron a minute, track gets sent out to get laid, and pickaxes get crafted by the dozen. District 3 is the main processing facility within Oberon, and is second to none for quality.

Mining Districts
Districts 4-273 are mines and towns of various sizes and depths strewn around the [[Whitefall Mountains]], ranging from ancient mines going down miles into the earth's crust to a small town of [[Dwarves]] that are mining a vein of silver. These districts are connected to smaller rail lines that are connected to the main network, with only the largest districts getting individual stops on the main rail line. Districts are numbered by age, so the lower the number, the older the district.
