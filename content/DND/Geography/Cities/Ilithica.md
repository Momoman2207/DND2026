Ilithica is the capitol of the [[Humans]] and [[Elves]], and is the largest above ground city on the continent of [[Adrios]]. Ilithica is home to the royal palace, as well as many of the central corporations that make up the world of elf and man. Ilithica is a massive trade partner with the [[Dwarves]], as they provide gold and silver to be used as currency, but also steel, a vital component in the newly emerging (to [[humans]] and [[Elves]]) industrial revolution. In response, they provide materials unable to be collected within the mountains, such as textiles, sand, wood, and other such commodities.
