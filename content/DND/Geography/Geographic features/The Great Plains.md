The Great Plains is a massive highland biome interspersed with the occasional walled city. The region is run by warbands of [[Orcs]], who pillage and burn whatever they can, always looking for a good scrap. This has left the region relatively underdevelopped, as trade networks of any kind, be train or road seem to be completely useless in the face of the wandering warbands. The cities that do exist here are either on the waterfront or new developments from airship travel, and even with airship travel, long trips across the plains dont work due to lack of fuel infastructure in the sparce plains.
